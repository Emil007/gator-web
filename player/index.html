gator

dynamic SM83 · your ROM · native flipper controls

Load your .gb

Bring your own ROM (.gb / .gbc / .zip). JP, US/EU, and Beta all work. Nothing is uploaded.

Local roms/

Runtime

Fetch → decode → execute each instruction from the loaded ROM in the browser. Timing uses DMG-ish cycles (DIV, TIMA, LY/STAT, interrupts) and a software PPU.

Controls

D-Pad = left · A/B = right + plunger. Mobile: bottom Start / Exit; flippers appear after the title.
